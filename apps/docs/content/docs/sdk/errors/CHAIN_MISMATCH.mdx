**Class** [`WalletError`](/docs/sdk/errors#walleterror) · **Code** `CHAIN_MISMATCH` · **Package** `@ensforge/sdk`

## What happened

The wallet is on chain ?, expected ?.

## What to show users

| Title | Message | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wrong network | Your wallet is connected to a different network. | Switch networks in your wallet. |

## How to fix

Switch the wallet's network, then retry.

## Retry safety

Retrying the same call fails the same way until the cause is fixed (`retryable: false`).
