**Class** [`HcaError`](/docs/sdk/errors#hcaerror) · **Code** `HCA_UNAVAILABLE` · **Package** `@ensforge/hca`

## What happened

This chain has no ENS HCA deployment.

## What to show users

| Title | Message | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Network not supported | ENS accounts are not available on this network. | Switch to Ethereum mainnet or Sepolia. |

## How to fix

Use mainnet or Sepolia, or pass a deployment with an `hca` group.

## Retry safety

Retrying the same call fails the same way until the cause is fixed (`retryable: false`).
