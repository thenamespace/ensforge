**Class** [`TransactionError`](/docs/sdk/errors#transactionerror) · **Code** `NONCE_CONFLICT` · **Package** `@ensforge/sdk`

## What happened

The transaction nonce conflicts with another transaction.

## What to show users

| Title | Message | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transaction conflict | Another transaction from your wallet is pending. | Wait for it to confirm, then try again. |

## How to fix

Wait for pending transactions to confirm, then retry.

## Retry safety

Retrying the same call can succeed (`retryable: true`).
