**Class** [`UnauthorizedError`](/docs/sdk/errors#unauthorizederror) · **Code** `MISSING_ROLES` · **Package** `@ensforge/sdk`

## What happened

The account is missing the roles this change needs.

## What to show users

| Title | Message | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permission needed | Your account is not allowed to make this change to this name. | Ask the owner for permission. |

## How to fix

Ask the name's owner to grant the roles, or send from the owner's account.
