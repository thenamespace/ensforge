**Class** [`UnauthorizedError`](/docs/sdk/errors#unauthorizederror) · **Code** `TRANSFER_DISALLOWED` · **Package** `@ensforge/sdk`

## What happened

This name cannot be transferred right now.

## What to show users

| Title | Message | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transfer blocked | This name cannot be transferred with the roles held on it. |  |

## How to fix

The owner lacks the role to transfer it, or other accounts hold roles on it (revoke them, or transfer with `unsafe: true`); `requirements` says which.

## Retry safety

Retrying the same call fails the same way until the cause is fixed (`retryable: false`).
