**Write** · Stable · Since 1.0.0

Removes a subname. Resolves once the transaction is submitted.

## When to use

Use to take back a name you gave out, or to drop your own subname. Use [`removeSync`](/docs/sdk/reference/actions/subname/remove#wait-for-the-receipt-with-removesync) to wait for the receipt.

## Details

- ENSv2: `unregister` in the parent's subregistry, which also clears the child's resolver and
  subregistry so nothing carries over to a later owner.
- ENSv1: owner and resolver are set to zero, from the parent when the signer owns it or is
  its operator, otherwise as the child's owner. Under an unwrapped parent the registry clears a wrapped
  child, which unwraps it.

<Callout type="warn" title="Gotchas">

- Emancipated children (ENSv1 `parentCannotControl`, or an emancipated ENSv2 subregistry)
  cannot be removed by their parent.

</Callout>
