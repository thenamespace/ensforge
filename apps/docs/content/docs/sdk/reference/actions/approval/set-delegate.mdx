**Write** · Stable · Since 1.0.0

Approves or revokes a delegate who may edit a name's records on its public resolver. Resolves once the transaction is submitted.

## When to use

Use to let another account manage a profile on a PublicResolver. Use [`setDelegateSync`](/docs/sdk/reference/actions/approval/set-delegate#wait-for-the-receipt-with-setdelegatesync) to wait for the receipt.

## Wait for the receipt with `setDelegateSync`

Approves or revokes a delegate who may edit a name's records on its public resolver. Resolves once the receipt is in.

Use when the next step depends on the change being on-chain.

`approval.setDelegateSync` takes the same options plus [`confirmations` and `timeout`](/docs/sdk/reference/client#sync-options).

Resolves to `Write.SyncResult`. Receipts plus the submitted hashes and plan.
