**Write** · Stable · Since 1.0.0

Resets the ENSv1 registry owner (manager) of an unwrapped `.eth` name, as its registrant. Resolves once the transaction is submitted.

## When to use

Use to take over the manager after receiving a `.eth` registrar token, or to repair a lost manager. Use [`reclaimSync`](/docs/sdk/reference/actions/name/reclaim#wait-for-the-receipt-with-reclaimsync) to wait for the receipt.

## Wait for the receipt with `reclaimSync`

Resets the ENSv1 registry owner (manager) of an unwrapped `.eth` name, as its registrant. Resolves once the receipt is in.

Use when the next step depends on the change being on-chain.

`name.reclaimSync` takes the same options plus [`confirmations` and `timeout`](/docs/sdk/reference/client#sync-options).

Resolves to `Write.SyncResult`. Receipts plus the submitted hashes and plan.
