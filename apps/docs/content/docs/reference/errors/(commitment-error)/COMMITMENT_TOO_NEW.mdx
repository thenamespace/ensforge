## What happened

The registration commitment is not ready yet.

## What to show users

| Title | Message | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Almost ready | Registration needs a short wait after the first step. | Wait a minute, then continue. |

## How to fix

Wait for the minimum commitment age, then register.

## Retry safety

Retrying the same call can succeed (`retryable: true`).

## Actions that throw it

| Action | When |
| --- | --- |
| [`registration.commit`](/docs/reference/sdk/registration/commit) | for a resumed commitment outside its window. |
