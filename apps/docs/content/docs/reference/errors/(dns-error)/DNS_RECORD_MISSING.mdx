## What happened

The `_ens` TXT record is missing.

## What to show users

| Title | Message | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DNS record missing | The required DNS TXT record was not found. | Add the TXT record at your DNS provider, then retry. |

## How to fix

Add a TXT record `_ens.<name>` with the value "a=\<owner address\>".

## Retry safety

Retrying the same call fails the same way until the cause is fixed (`retryable: false`).
