## What happened

The DNSSEC proof could not be built or verified.

## What to show users

| Title | Message | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DNSSEC problem | Your domain's DNSSEC setup could not be verified. | Check that DNSSEC is enabled at your DNS provider. |

## How to fix

Check that DNSSEC is enabled for the domain.
